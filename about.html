About

A quiet coffee-plantation stay 13 km from Madikeri — birdsong at dawn, Kodava flavours on the sitout, and Coorg’s best day trips close by.

A home away from home

Nestled amidst a lush coffee plantation, Chombuka offers a serene escape from the everyday. Wake to the melodies of chirping birds and enjoy proximity to Coorg's most sought-after tourist attractions. Our estate invites you to savor the quiet of nature walks, indulge in traditional Kodava cuisine right from the comfort of your private sitout, or simply revel in the tranquility that surrounds you.
