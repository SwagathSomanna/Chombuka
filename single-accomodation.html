Accomodation

Chombuka is set in Madikeri, 13 km from Madikeri Fort and 13 km from Raja Seat. This property offers access to a balcony, free private parking and free WiFi. Abbi Falls is 17 km from the homestay.

The homestay comes with a flat-screen TV. Towels and bed linen are featured in the homestay.

The nearest airport is Kannur International Airport, 93 km from the homestay.
